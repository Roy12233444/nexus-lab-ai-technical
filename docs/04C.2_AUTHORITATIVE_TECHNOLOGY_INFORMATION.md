This register establishes the **authoritative technological baseline** for Nexus LAB AI, compressing the raw inventory in `04C.1_TECHNOLOGY_KNOWLEDGE_INVENTORY.md` into an accurate, defensible, and structured technology register.

### Core Epistemic Principles:

1. **Compress Information, Not Truth:** Remove raw inventory redundancies while strictly preserving epistemic boundaries, evidence levels, and maturity states.
2. **Capability-First, Not Shopping-List:** Present technology as coherent capability systems (Execution, Memory, Governance, Reasoning, Space OS) rather than a generic catalogue of third-party tools.
3. **Decoupled Three-Tier Hierarchy:**
   ```text
   TECHNOLOGY (Underlying Architecture / Mechanism)
         ↓
   CAPABILITY (Systemic Problem Solved)
         ↓
   NEXUS PROJECT (Representative Implementation Vehicle)
   ```
4. **Strict Project Independence:** Technology association does NOT imply runtime operational coupling. Shared architectural concepts (e.g., state persistence or safety gating) do not create an unverified pipeline between projects.
5. **Decoupled Four-Axis Truth Model:**
   - **Maturity:** `IMPLEMENTED` | `EXPERIMENTAL` | `RESEARCH` | `PLANNED` | `SUPPORTING`
   - **Evidence:** `UNKNOWN` | `DOCUMENTED` | `REPORTED` | `OBSERVED` | `VERIFIED`

6. **Separation of Nexus Inventions vs Supporting Dependencies:** Distinguish proprietary first-principles implementations (e.g. Chiranjeevi persistence core, ARIES crash recovery) from standard open-source dependencies (Python, Rust, ZeroMQ, Docker).
