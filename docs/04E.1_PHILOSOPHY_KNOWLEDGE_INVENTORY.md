### 01.02 — Epistemology (_Pramāṇa-Śāstra_) Inventory

The research establishes an epistemological foundation centered around the _tripuṭī_ (knower, object, means of knowledge) and the generation of _pramā_ (valid cognition):

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    TRIPUṬĪ (KNOWING TRIAD)                   │
├─────────────────┬─────────────────────────┬─────────────────┤
│ Pramātṛ         │ Prameya                 │ Pramāṇa         │
│ (Subject/Agent) │ (Object/World State)    │ (Epistemic Tool)│
└─────────────────┴─────────────────────────┴─────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
                    PRAMĀ (Valid Cognition)
                               │
        ┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
        ▼                                             ▼
  Svataḥ Prāmāṇya                               Parataḥ Prāmāṇya
  (Intrinsic Validity — Mīmāṃsā)                (Extrinsic Verification — Nyāya)
  [Valid upon causal generation;                [Awareness arises first; verified
   invalidated only by defeaters]                externally via pragmatic success]
```

#### Core Epistemological Primitives:

1. **Pratyakṣa (Perception):**
   - _Realist Definition (Nyāya):_ Sense-object contact generating non-erratic, non-verbal, determinate cognition.
   - _Two-Stage Processing:_ Indeterminate raw signal (_nirvikalpaka_) $\to$ Structured determinate semantic representation (_savikalpaka_).
   - _Nominalist Definition (Buddhist):_ Pure momentary point-instant (_svalakṣaṇa_) free from conceptual fabrication (_kalpanāpoḍham_).
2. **Anumāna (Inference):**
   - _Pañcāvayava (5-Member Syllogism):_ Proposition (_pratijñā_) $\to$ Ground/Evidence (_hetu_) $\to$ Universal Concomitance Rule with Example (_udāharaṇa_) $\to$ Application (_upanaya_) $\to$ Conclusion (_nigamana_).
   - _Vyāpti (Invariable Concomitance):_ Empirical, unconditional pervasion binding reason to conclusion.
   - _Hetvābhāsa (Fallacies):_ Unproven ground, counterbalanced ground, unanchored ground, or contradictory ground.
3. **Śabda (Testimony):**
   - Relies on _āpta-vākya_ (statement of a verified, competent, defect-free authority).
   - Extraction of meaning (_śābdabodha_) requires 4 linguistic conditions: Syntactic expectancy (_ākāṅkṣā_), Semantic fitness (_yogyatā_), Contiguity (_sannidhi_), and Contextual intent (_tātparya_).
4. **Khyātivāda (Error & Hallucination Theories):**
   - _Anyathākhyāti (Nyāya):_ Mislocation and real-attribute projection onto a different locus.
   - _Asatkhyāti (Madhyamaka):_ Apprehension of the non-existent.
   - _Anirvacanīyakhyāti (Advaita):_ Cognition of an ontologically indeterminate appearance.
   - _Akhyāti (Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā):_ Non-discrimination between present perception and retrieved memory.

### 01.03 — Cognition (_Antaḥkaraṇa_) Inventory

The research isolates the multi-layer functional architecture of the internal cognitive organ (_antaḥkaraṇa_):

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              ANTAḤKARAṆA (INTERNAL COGNITIVE STACK)         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. MANAS (Sensory Synthesizer & Router)                     │
│    - Gathers raw inputs from indriyas                       │
│    - Performs initial doubt/deliberation (saṃśaya-saṅkalpa) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. AHAṂKĀRA (Ego / Frame of Reference Operator)             │
│    - Binds percepts to self-referential frame (abhimāna)    │
│    - Establishes subjective perspective ("This is for me")  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. BUDDHI (Intellect, Determinative Judgment, Reason)       │
│    - Evaluates, classifies, resolves ambiguity (adhyavasāya)│
│    - Issues deliberate decisions and executive commands     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. CITTA (Durable Memory & Latent Trace Store)              │
│    - Stores latent impressions (saṃskāras) and traits (vāsanā)│
│    - Powers memory retrieval (smṛti) and recognition         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Key Cognitive Dynamics:

- **Cittavṛtti (Cognitive Fluctuations):** Dynamic modifications of the mind (valid knowledge, error, conceptualization, sleep, memory).
- **Saṃskāra & Smṛti (Latent Traces & Memory):** Experiences leave persistent latent impressions that activate upon contextual resonance, driving pattern completion and associative recall.
- **Pratyabhijñā (Recognition):** The cognitive fusion of direct sensory perception with retrieved latent memory traces (_"This is that same entity"_).

### 01.04 — Agency (_Kartṛtva_) Inventory

The research deconstructs agency, volition, and action into distinct structural models:

1. **Material Realism Agency (Sāṃkhya Functionalism):**
   - All executive functions, planning, and action belong strictly to the material, mechanistic substrate (_Prakṛti_ / _Buddhi_).
   - Consciousness (_Puruṣa_) is a passive witness (_sākṣin_), devoid of physical causality. The illusion of conscious agency arises from reflective superimposition.
2. **Causal Volition Agency (Nyāya Realism):**
   - Action requires a strict causal chain: Valid Cognition (_jñāna_) $\to$ Desire/Aversion (_icchā_ / _dveṣa_) $\to$ Volitional Effort (_prayatna_) $\to$ Physical Motor Action (_kṛti_ / _ceṣṭā_).
3. **Reflective Sovereignty (Kashmir Śaiva Agency):**
   - True agency (_kartṛtva_) requires dynamic freedom (_svātantrya_), self-awareness (_vimarśa_), and deliberate choice rather than mere mechanical determinism.
4. **Karma Mechanics & Agency Constraints:**
   - Action (_karma_) generates latent potential (_apūrva_ / _adṛṣṭa_), creating structural constraints on future action spaces without negating immediate volitional choice (_puruṣakāra_).

### 01.05 — Dharma & Responsibility Inventory

The research firmly establishes that _Dharma_ in classical Indian thought is not a static list of moral rules or a synonym for legal compliance:

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 DHARMIC RESPONSIBILITY MATRIX                │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ Sāmānya-Dharma               │ Svadharma                    │
│ (Universal Invariants)       │ (Contextual Role Duties)     │
│ - Non-injury (Ahiṃsā)        │ - Epistemic role             │
│ - Truthfulness (Satya)       │ - Operational boundary       │
│ - Integrity (Asteya)         │ - Domain-specific duty       │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
         DHARMA-SAṄKAṬA (Normative Conflict Resolution)
          - Priority hierarchies across conflicting duties
          - Contextual constraint optimization
          - Mitigation of selfish bias (Niṣkāma Karma)
```

#### Core Normative Primitives:

- **Adhikāra (Competence & Qualification):** An agent is only bound by and authorized to execute actions for which it possesses the prerequisite knowledge, capacity, and operational authorization.
- **Dharma-saṅkaṭa (Dilemma Resolution):** Situations where two valid normative injunctions conflict directly. Resolution requires higher-order contextual reasoning, hierarchical priority rules, and minimal systemic harm (_ahiṃsā_).
- **Phala & Hetu (Consequence & Intentional Causality):** Actions are judged not merely by outward execution, but by underlying intentionality (_cetanā_) and resulting systemic impact (_karmaphala_).

### 01.09 — Evidentiary Taxonomy & State Baseline

The inventory classifies the current evidentiary standing across all philosophical-computational concepts:

```text
DOCUMENTED   → Concept researched, historical sources cataloged, and theoretical formulation written.
FORMALIZED   → Translated into symbolic logic, mathematical constraints, or schema definitions.
EXPERIMENTAL → Prototype code or benchmark evaluation constructed in laboratory testing.
IMPLEMENTED  → Functional software modules integrated into laboratory repositories.
VERIFIED     → Reproducible empirical metrics published and independently audited.
UNKNOWN      → Insufficient empirical data or unverified theoretical conjecture.
```

- **90% of philosophical integrations are currently `DOCUMENTED` or `RESEARCH` hypotheses.**
- **0% of philosophical integrations are claimed as universally `VERIFIED` final solutions.**
- **Absence of empirical proof is explicitly designated as `DOCUMENTED HYPOTHESIS` or `UNKNOWN`.**
