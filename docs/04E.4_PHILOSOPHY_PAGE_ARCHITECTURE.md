## 4. Architectural Rules & Epistemic Boundaries

1. **Rule A (Unidirectional Derivation):**  
   $\text{Classical Philosophical Concept} \to \text{Modern Nexus Interpretation} \to \text{Testable AI Hypothesis} \to \text{Computational Architecture}$.  
   The page strictly prohibits reverse-engineering ancient authority onto pre-existing software.
2. **Rule B (Hypothesis Status Preservation):**  
   Every AI hypothesis rendered on the page must prominently display its `HYPOTHESIS` status and evidence level (`DOCUMENTED` / `EXPERIMENTAL`).
3. **Rule C (Controlled Mapping Visibility):**  
   Relationship badges must visibly indicate whether a mapping is `DIRECT`, `RESEARCH`, or `ANALOGY`.
4. **Rule D (Mandatory Negative Boundaries):**  
   Every public concept must expose its negative non-claims, ensuring visitors clearly understand what Nexus LAB AI does _not_ claim.
5. **Rule E (Accessibility & Semantic DOM):**  
   Exactly one `<h1>` in `PhilosophyHeroSection`, sequential `<h2>` section titles, accessible `<section>` landmarks with unique IDs, and `aria-hidden="true"` on decorative topology visuals.
