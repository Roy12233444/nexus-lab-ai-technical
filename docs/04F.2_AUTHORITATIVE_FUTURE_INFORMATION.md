## 4. Epistemic Invariants & Anti-Inflation Rules

1. **COSMOS Spaceflight Firewall**:
   - `FDIR-006` is permanently locked to status `LONG_HORIZON` and evidence `DOCUMENTED`.

2. **Anti-Prediction Planning Constraints**:
   - Timeframes (`0–2 Years`, `2–5 Years`, `5+ Years`) are typed planning horizons, not deterministic completion deadlines or commercial guarantees.
3. **Mandatory Non-Claims**:
   - Every `FutureDirection` object must contain at least one explicit negative boundary in `nonClaims[]`.
4. **Mandatory Provenance Anchors**:
   - Every future direction and open problem must maintain explicit source traceability via `sourceRefs[]` back to 04A–04E authoritative dossiers.
