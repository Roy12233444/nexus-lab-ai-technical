## 2. Visual Philosophy & Core DNA

### The Anti-Cyberpunk Invariant

The Future Track deliberately avoids generic, dystopian "AI sci-fi" tropes:

- ❌ **No black backgrounds or neon glows**
- ❌ **No glowing blue holographic brains or energy beams**
- ❌ **No humanoid robots, cyborgs, or fantasy starships**
- ❌ **No fake mission-control dashboards or synthetic interfaces**

### The Scientific Institutional Aesthetic

The visual language reflects a serious, world-class research institution:

- ✅ **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) / Light Slate Canvas**: Maximizes reading comfort, intellectual clarity, and precision.
- ✅ **Scientifically Grounded Earth & Orbital Mechanics**: Realistic atmospheric scattering, accurate continental proportions, and subtle technical geometry.
- ✅ **Human Agency as Collective Progress**: Humans are depicted as diverse researchers, engineers, and builders working intentionally—never a lone "superhero".
- ✅ **Generous Negative Space & High-Contrast Typography**: Content breathes naturally with clean borders, subtle elevation (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-2xs`), and monospaced technical metadata pills.

## 5. Responsive Behavior & Component Treatments

### Layout Rules

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**:
  - Sections 02 & 05: Asymmetric 2-column layout (Text content in col-span-7, Editorial image card in col-span-5).
  - Section 08: Side-by-side Earth Foundation (1:1) + Orbital Horizon (16:9) dual-card container.
  - Sections 01 & 10: Full-width hero containers with centered or horizon-aligned imagery.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: Stacked column with text first, followed by responsive full-width image container with aspect-ratio preservation.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Text-first reading flow, image rendered at full width with rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`) and subtle border (`border-slate-200`).

### Image Container Styling

- `rounded-2xl` with `border border-slate-200` and `bg-slate-50`.
- `object-cover` or `object-contain` depending on aspect ratio.
- Native `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"` on below-the-fold assets (Images 2 through 7).
- Standardized caption box underneath with monospaced figure identifier.
