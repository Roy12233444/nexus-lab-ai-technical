        ↓

The Autonomous Systems Stress Matrix tabulating challenges vs pressures.

        ↓

Nexus program, philosophy, and future track alignment.

STAGE 9: EPISTEMIC RESTRAINT (Section 09)
Global negative non-claims and isolated quarantined claims.
        ↓
STAGE 10: SYNTHESIS (Section 10)
The open frontier closing statement and cross-route laboratory gateways.

### Detailed Section Blueprints

#### Section 01: `challenge-hero`

- **Title:** The Hard Problems Behind Autonomous Intelligence
- **Eyebrow:** Nexus LAB AI Problem Frontier
- **Purpose:** Establish the central thesis: persistent autonomous agency creates compounding failure modes that cannot be solved by compute scale alone.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeHeroSection`
- **Container Width:** `full` | **Background:** `primary`
- **Data Binding:** `getChallengeContent()`
- **Visual Role:** Hero conceptual orientation slot framing systemic autonomy pressure.
- **Forbidden Content:** Marketing claims that any challenge is solved; speculative delivery dates.

#### Section 02: `challenge-pressure-model`

- **Title:** The Seven Pressure Dimensions of Autonomy
- **Eyebrow:** Systemic Physics
- **Purpose:** Introduce the 7 foundational pressure vectors (Time, Uncertainty, Failure, Scale, Resource Limits, Responsibility, Environment Shift).
- **Component Type:** `ChallengePressureModelSection`
- **Container Width:** `wide` | **Background:** `secondary`
- **Data Binding:** `getPressureDimensions()`
- **Visual Role:** Structured topology layout visualizing compounding vectors of systemic stress.
- **Forbidden Content:** Inventing pressure dimensions outside the canonical 7.

#### Section 03: `challenge-frontier`

- **Title:** The Eight Canonical Engineering Challenges
- **Eyebrow:** Problem Landscape
- **Purpose:** Present all 8 canonical challenges (`CH-001` to `CH-008`) with pressure vectors, evidence states, and problem statements.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeFrontierSection`
- **Container Width:** `wide` | **Background:** `primary`
- **Data Binding:** `getAllChallenges()`
- **Visual Role:** Eight-card diagnostic grid displaying canonical challenge profiles.
- **Forbidden Content:** Omitting any canonical challenge; promoting theoretical tiers to proven facts.

#### Section 04: `challenge-anatomy`

- **Title:** Deep Failure Anatomy & Technical Dimensions
- **Eyebrow:** Mechanistic Analysis
- **Purpose:** Deconstruct why autonomy escalates difficulty, detailing specific failure modes (e.g. Context Catastrophe, Cascading Retry Loops) and concrete technical dimensions.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeAnatomySection`
- **Container Width:** `content` | **Background:** `secondary`
- **Data Binding:** `getAllChallenges()`
- **Visual Role:** Deep technical breakdown containers with structured failure mode callouts.
- **Forbidden Content:** Abstract hand-waving without concrete technical dimensions.

#### Section 05: `challenge-cascade`

- **Title:** Systemic Cascading Failure Dynamics
- **Eyebrow:** Failure Interaction
- **Purpose:** Illustrate how isolated failures in memory, epistemics, or recovery compound into systemic deadlocks and multi-agent coordination collapse.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeCascadeSection`
- **Container Width:** `content` | **Background:** `primary`
- **Data Binding:** `getChallengeContent()`
- **Visual Role:** Sequential cascade graph demonstrating compound error propagation.
- **Forbidden Content:** Claiming failure cascades are fully deterministic or universal.

#### Section 06: `challenge-matrix`

- **Title:** Autonomous Systems Stress Matrix
- **Eyebrow:** Diagnostic Cross-Evaluation
- **Purpose:** Tabulate all 8 challenges across the 7 pressure dimensions as a diagnostic instrument.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeMatrixSection`
- **Container Width:** `wide` | **Background:** `secondary`
- **Data Binding:** `getAllChallenges()`
- **Visual Role:** High-density truth table matrix with interactive pressure vector highlighting.
- **Forbidden Content:** Mismatched pressure vector assignments conflicting with canonical registry.

#### Section 07: `challenge-open-questions`

- **Title:** Open Scientific & Systems Research Questions
- **Eyebrow:** Unsolved Frontier
- **Purpose:** Transparently present open mathematical and systems research questions across all 8 challenges.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeOpenQuestionsSection`
- **Container Width:** `content` | **Background:** `primary`
- **Data Binding:** `getAllChallenges()`
- **Visual Role:** Editorial research docket with structured question scopes and investigation tracks.
- **Forbidden Content:** Hiding open questions to make problems appear artificially solved.

#### Section 08: `challenge-alignment`

- **Title:** Nexus Systems & Cross-Track Research Alignment
- **Eyebrow:** Institutional Intersections
- **Purpose:** Map every challenge to its governing Nexus Programs, Philosophy Concepts, and Future Directions.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeAlignmentSection`
- **Container Width:** `wide` | **Background:** `secondary`
- **Data Binding:** `getChallengeCrossTrackMappings()`
- **Visual Role:** Cross-track relationship matrix linking challenges to architectural anchors.
- **Forbidden Content:** Claiming research alignment equals production completion.

#### Section 09: `challenge-boundaries`

- **Title:** Global Negative Boundaries & Quarantined Non-Claims
- **Eyebrow:** Epistemic Discipline
- **Purpose:** Enforce institutional truth boundaries: explicitly display negative non-claims and isolated quarantined claims.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeBoundariesSection`
- **Container Width:** `content` | **Background:** `primary`
- **Data Binding:** `getGlobalChallengeNegativeBoundaries()`
- **Visual Role:** High-contrast boundary container with quarantined warning block and strike-through text.
- **Forbidden Content:** Removing or softening explicit negative boundaries.

#### Section 10: `challenge-closing`

- **Title:** The Open Frontier: Engineering Sovereign Resilient Systems
- **Eyebrow:** Synthesis & Trajectory
- **Purpose:** Synthesize the challenge narrative and provide gateway navigation to Research, Tech, Programs, Philosophy, and Future.
- **Component Type:** `ChallengeClosingSection`
- **Container Width:** `content` | **Background:** `secondary`
- **Data Binding:** `getChallengeContent()`
- **Visual Role:** Reflective closing statement with interactive navigation gateways.
- **Forbidden Content:** Triumphalist claims of total victory over AI challenges.

## 4. Information Geometries & Cognitive Layout Rhythm

To prevent visual fatigue, the 10 sections cycle through distinct information geometries:

- **Hero:** Full-bleed institutional aperture with ambient atmospheric depth.
- **Pressure Model:** Multi-node clustered topology layout.
- **Frontier:** 2-column and 4-column responsive diagnostic cards.
- **Anatomy:** Tabbed and expandable mechanistic dossiers.
- **Cascade:** Linear directed flow diagram with branch callouts.
- **Matrix:** Dense structured data table with column vector filtering.
- **Open Questions:** Two-column editorial research cards.
- **Alignment:** 4-column relational alignment matrix.
- **Boundaries:** Boxed quarantine warning container with red strike-through accents.
- **Closing:** Centered monolithic typography with modular navigation cards.
