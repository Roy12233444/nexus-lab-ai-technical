## Stage 03: Literature & Evidence Investigation (BEL-NEW-03)

This investigation audited empirical computer science, cognitive psychology, and software engineering literature to extract verified facts and failure modes.

### A. Supporting Empirical Evidence
1. **Developer Productivity & Task Compression (Peng et al., 2023; Dell'Acqua et al., Harvard/BCG, 2023):**
   - Controlled trials on complex knowledge tasks demonstrated a **55.8% reduction in task completion time** for software development, with the largest performance gains concentrated in the 25th–50th percentile of practitioners.
2. **Accelerated Exploration of Combinatorial Spaces (AlphaFold / GNoME / DeepMind, 2023):**
   - In material science and structural biology, AI did not replace wet-lab verification; rather, it reduced the candidate hypothesis space from $10^{12}$ possibilities to a prioritized list of $10^2$, increasing experimental yield by orders of magnitude.
3. **Cognitive Externalization & Artifact Durability (Clark & Chalmers, "The Extended Mind", 1998):**
   - Externalizing intermediate reasoning into persistent, typed artifacts reduces cognitive load on human working memory, allowing small teams to maintain mental coherence over enterprise-scale architectures.

### B. Opposing Evidence & Documented Failure Modes
1. **The "Jagged Frontier" & Illusion of Competence (Dell'Acqua et al., 2023):**
   - When AI is used outside its capability frontier, human accuracy drops by **19 percentage points**. Users develop unjustified trust, blindly accepting plausible-sounding errors.
2. **Technical Debt Amplification & Code Quality Decay (GitClear Research, 2024):**
   - Analysis of 153M lines of code revealed a dramatic increase in "code churn" (code replaced within 2 weeks of being written) and a drop in refactoring and DRY principles, indicating that rapid generation often leads to rapid decay.
3. **The Verification Bottleneck (METR / Model Evaluation & Threat Research, 2024):**
   - As generation cost approaches zero, **the cognitive cost of verifying correctness dominates the system**. If verification tools are absent, the entire acceleration evaporates into debugging time.
